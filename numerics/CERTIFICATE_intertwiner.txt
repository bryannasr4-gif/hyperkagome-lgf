RESULT: ALL CHECKS PASS.
The determinant character of M -- the quadratic character of
v^2 = (1-4t)(1-5t)(1-9t) -- is the ONLY obstruction to a rational intertwiner
with Sym^2(V_2) in projective normal form.  It makes Hom(M, Sym^2(V_2)) = 0 over
Q(t) in both directions, it is removed by the single conjugation M_v = v M v^{-1},
and over M_v the intertwiner is the ORDER-ONE operator T = rho_0 + rho_1 d/dt.
Being of order two, the character is invisible at the symmetric-square level,
which is why the determinant obstruction is absent between Sym^2(M) and
Sym^4(V_2) (their homomorphy was reported by J.-M. Maillard, private
communication, and is NOT certified by this script) while M and Sym^2(V_2)
are provably not homomorphic.
